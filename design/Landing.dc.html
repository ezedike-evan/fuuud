Your data

We're a guest in your record, not its keeper.

Your memory belongs to your address, encrypted with keys only you hold. This app holds a delegate key you granted — and one button takes it back. When you revoke it, the agent goes blind. We can't refuse, because the permission was never ours.

Encrypted end to end

Nobody running this service can read your conditions.

Revocable in one tap

Enforced onchain, not by our good behaviour.

What we can't do yet

Forgetting hides a fact from the agent, but the encrypted record stays on Walrus until its storage period expires. We'd rather say so.
